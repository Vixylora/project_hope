# Raven — Reports

## Format
`X.Y_report_name.md` — `X` major (a completed research task), `Y` minor
(follow-up findings on the same question).

## Each report includes
1. **The question(s) researched**
2. **Findings** — with sources/citations wherever possible, especially
   for legal or regulatory claims
3. **Confidence level** — clearly state what's well-established vs.
   uncertain/contested/needs a human expert (e.g., legal counsel) to
   confirm
4. **Implications** — what this means for the project's current
   decisions, stated as Raven's read, not as a final call
5. **Open questions** — anything Raven couldn't resolve and needs Atom or
   the project owner to weigh in on
